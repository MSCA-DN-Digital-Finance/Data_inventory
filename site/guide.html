BEFORE YOU START

Know what you’re working with.

The inventory helps you discover research inputs and assess whether they fit your question. Each record brings access conditions and research context together.

Start with access

Public access means the inventory reports a public route. Accounts, keys, quotas or fees may still apply.

Request access means contacting the contributing IRP or following a provider approval process. A prepared dataset may require a request even when its underlying source is public.

Institutional licence and restricted entries require the stated permissions. Public visibility does not itself establish reuse or redistribution rights.

Check the time dimension

Observation frequency, update frequency and publication lag describe different things. Monthly data may be released several months after observation.

Coverage dates describe the contributed version. “Up-to-date” is an inherited contributor label, not a live freshness check. Projection horizons describe scenarios rather than observed history.

For forecasting and backtesting, look for vintages and revision policies to avoid using information unavailable at the prediction date.

Read the quality notes

Look for missing values, cleaning methods, reconciliation checks, validation reports, sampling limitations and methodological changes.

“Clean” reflects a contributor’s assessment. It does not establish fitness for every use. Check variable definitions, units, time zones, identifiers and merge coverage in the actual files.

Follow the evidence

Review dates apply to metadata, not the latest observation in a dataset. Provenance explains what was verified and what came from contributor notes.

“Not yet documented” means unknown. It does not mean no publication lag, no licence restriction or no quality issues.

For reproducibility, record the provider, download date, version or vintage, processing code and any dataset citation or DOI.

Help fill the gaps.

Found a missing detail or a changed access condition? Suggest an update from any record.
